# Code of Conduct — Heros PDF

## Our promise

We pledge to make Heros PDF a welcoming, harassment-free home for everyone —
regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender, experience, nationality,
religion, or identity. **Be kind. Be clear. Be fast.**

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming, inclusive language. Assume good intent first.
- Give actionable code review (`"try X because Y"`, not `"this is bad"`).
- Respect the 100%-local privacy mission; never propose tracking users.
- Credit others; don't claim their work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, sexualized language, doxxing, or threats — in issues,
  PRs, discussions, or DMs about this project.
- Deliberately uploading malicious code, exfiltrating test PDFs, or sneaking
  in network calls.
- Spam, 광고, or off-topic promotion.

## Enforcement

1. **Warning** — private message, explain, ask to fix.
2. **Temporary ban** — 7–30 days from issues/PRs/discussions.
3. **Permanent ban** — for severe or repeated violations.

Report privately: open a draft email to the maintainer via your GitHub profile
contact, or tag `@Ahmedbakr78` with `[CoC]` in a private discussion. All reports
are confidential. Retaliation = immediate ban.
